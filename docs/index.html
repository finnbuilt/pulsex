Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Is PulseX truly 100% free forever? +
Yes. PulseX has zero subscriptions, zero paywalls, and zero upsells. It is open-source under the MIT License, which gives you complete freedom to use it, distribute it, fork it, and even commercially sell distributions.
Can I use PulseX without paying any API costs? +
Yes! You can connect PulseX to Google Gemini's free tier via Google AI Studio, or run a local model like Llama 3.2 via Ollama on your computer for completely free, unlimited, private replies.
How does PulseX protect my privacy? +
PulseX operates under a strict BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) architecture. We run zero backend servers and zero telemetry scripts. When you generate a reply, the request goes directly from your browser to your selected AI provider's official endpoint over HTTPS.
Does PulseX ever auto-post or schedule tweets? +
Never. PulseX strictly complies with X's Developer Terms. It acts strictly as a composition assistant — you choose when to generate, you review the text, and you click "Reply" yourself.
Why are em-dashes (—) strictly banned? +
The em-dash paired with repetitive author greetings is the signature hallmark of lazy LLM slop. Real humans tweet in short, punchy sentence fragments with conversational syntax. PulseX enforces this cadence at the prompt and parser level.
